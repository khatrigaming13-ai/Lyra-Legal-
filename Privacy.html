1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Lyra#6709 Discord bot ("the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") and information processed through its features.

Discord independently processes information under Discord's own Privacy Policy. This policy describes processing performed by Lyra, not by Discord generally.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled in a server, the Bot may process the following categories of information:

  • Discord identifiers: user ID, server/guild ID, channel ID, voice channel ID, and message or interaction ID where required for a feature.
  • Profile information: username, display name, avatar information, and server nickname where available through Discord.
  • Server configuration: configured channels, roles, command preferences, music settings, permissions-related configuration, and similar settings.
  • Music and queue information: search queries, requested tracks, queue entries, playback state, media identifiers, and related information needed to provide music functionality.
  • Bot interaction data: commands, command options, interaction IDs, timestamps, and information needed to execute the requested command.
  • Technical information: limited diagnostic or error information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Bot.

3. Message Content

The Bot should process message content only when a feature technically requires it and when the Bot has the applicable Discord access. If message content is not required for a feature, it should not be intentionally stored by the Bot.

If the Bot uses Discord's privileged Message Content Intent, its actual access and use should match the Bot's documented functionality and Discord's applicable requirements.

4. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate Bot commands and features.
  • Process music searches and playback requests.
  • Remember server configuration and preferences.
  • Maintain queues and playback state when required.
  • Prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, maintain security, and improve reliability.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell personal information to advertisers.
  • We do not use Discord data for unrelated advertising purposes.
  • We do not intentionally collect passwords, Discord authentication tokens, or payment card details through the Bot.
  • Lyra is not designed to record or store private voice conversations.

Do not send sensitive personal information to the Bot through commands, messages, forms, or support channels unless a specific feature expressly requires it.

6. Voice Channels

Lyra may connect to Discord voice channels when users request music playback. Voice-channel functionality is used to provide the Bot's intended music features.

Lyra is not designed to record or store users' private voice conversations.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant feature, maintain security, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or maintain legitimate operational records.

Retention can vary by feature. Temporary diagnostic information may be retained for a shorter period, while server configuration may remain available while a server uses the Bot.

8. Data Sharing

We may disclose limited information:

  • To infrastructure or hosting providers that help operate the Bot, where necessary.
  • To third-party services required to provide music or media functionality.
  • When required by law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.
  • When necessary to protect the security, rights, or property of the Bot, its users, or others.

We do not sell personal information.

9. Discord and Third-Party Processing

Lyra runs through Discord's platform. Discord independently processes data according to its own policies. Third-party music or media providers may also process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

For more information, review Discord's Privacy Policy and Discord's Terms of Service.

10. Server Administrator Responsibilities

Server administrators decide whether to install the Bot and which features and permissions to enable. Administrators should configure music, logging, and other data-related features in a manner appropriate for their community and applicable laws.

11. Your Choices and Requests

Depending on the data involved and applicable law, you may request information about data associated with your Discord account or ask for deletion of Bot-held data.

Because Discord is a separate platform, requests concerning data controlled by Discord itself should be directed to Discord.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information handled by the Bot. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information through the Bot.

13. Children's Privacy

Lyra#6709 is a Discord application and is intended to be used in accordance with Discord's age requirements and policies. We do not knowingly request or intentionally collect additional sensitive information from children through the Bot.

14. International Processing

Depending on where the Bot and its infrastructure are hosted, information may be processed in countries other than your own. By using the Bot, you acknowledge that such processing may occur, subject to applicable law.

15. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Bot's features, data practices, infrastructure, or legal requirements change. The "Last updated" date will be revised when changes are published.
